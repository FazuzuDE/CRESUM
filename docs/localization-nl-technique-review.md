The treadmill walking and generic seated cable row guides were checked and needed no translation changes.

The Smith squat now distinguishes the lower safety stops from the hooks that secure the bar. The leg extension places the knees in line with the machine’s pivot axis and the pad above the ankles. The lying barbell triceps extension keeps the bar clear of the head throughout the lowering movement. The wide and ergonomic lat pulldown variants specify a slight, constant backward lean.
