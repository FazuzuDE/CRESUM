# Exercise Technique — content and source mapping

Checked 2026-10-12 against develop adb404c964687553613f064b41fec59647e0963b.

The approved mockup adds a read-only Technique sheet and full-screen placeholder view. Runtime content lives in `lib/exercise-technique`; it does not alter workout identity, prescriptions, measurement contracts or saved snapshots.

## Editorial method

Original concise English summaries of primary instructional material, not copied articles or claims that an organization endorsed CRESUM. Reference publisher/title/URL/check date are stored per source. Exact source exercises and CRESUM adaptations are distinguished: source examples may use different loading, support, attachments or grips. Common mistakes are corrective cues grounded in movement/control boundaries, not guarantees of safety or a ranking of injury risks. Machine placards govern their actual adjustment/release/safety systems; do not invent universal angles or settings.

Known unspecified legacy identities remain unspecified. Back Extension explicitly distinguishes hip-extension bench and seated lumbar-machine execution, rather than changing its saved meaning. Plank is a duration hold and Farmer’s Carry is distance; these descriptions do not add duration/distance logging support.

References were inspected through public pages, cached search excerpts and manufacturer manual text. Manufacturer manuals mirrored by ManualsLib/ManualMachine retain publisher attribution. ACE’s chest-technique PDF was retrievable as a cached excerpt but its direct browser-tool open failed; external link availability is not guaranteed. The old NSCA basics-manual URL returned 404 and was excluded from this new registry; the available university-hosted NSCA proficiency material supports the wide-pronated pulldown. No copyrighted photographs or text passages are imported. All guides remain offline even when source links fail.

A qualified coach/editor still needs to review adapted configurations before release. This is source-grounded general guidance, not an individualized assessment or independent clinical validation. Physical phone/Dynamic Type/VoiceOver/sheet acceptance remains outstanding. No production artwork exists; the placeholders make no movement demonstration, zoom or shared-image-transition claim.
